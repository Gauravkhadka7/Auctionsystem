LotHouse is a student-built online auction platform demonstrating a full bidding lifecycle: listing approval, live countdown bidding, automatic auction closing, winner selection, and payment/delivery tracking.

Built with Django, Bootstrap 5, and vanilla JavaScript as a portfolio and coursework project.

{% elif template_body == "how_it_works" %}
  1. Register as a bidder or seller and verify your email.
  2. Sellers list items with a starting price, reserve price, and auction window; admins approve listings before they go live.
  3. Bidders place bids in real time once the auction opens; every bid must beat the current price by at least the increment.
  4. Auctions close automatically when the timer hits zero — the highest bidder (above reserve) is declared the winner.
  5. Winners verify identity and submit payment; sellers arrange delivery once payment is confirmed.
